Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 August 2026 · Applies to Overload for iPhone and Apple Watch

Overload has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Your training data is stored on your device and is never sent anywhere unless you choose to export or share it yourself.

What Overload stores

Exercises, logged sets (weight, reps, date) and your settings are stored locally on your iPhone and shared with the Overload app on your paired Apple Watch. That data never leaves your devices on its own. There is no cloud sync and no backend service to send it to.

What Overload collects about you

Nothing. Overload contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting services and no third-party libraries of any kind. It does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not build a profile of you.

Apple Health

Saving to Apple Health is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, Overload writes your logged sets to Health as strength-training workouts. Overload never reads any data from Apple Health. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app, and data already written to Health stays under your control there.

Backups and exports

Backups and CSV exports are created only when you ask for one. The resulting file is handed to iOS, and where it goes — Files, iCloud Drive, email, another app — is entirely your choice. Overload has no visibility into where you send it.

Tips

Overload is free. Optional tips are one-time in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. Payment details are processed by Apple and are never seen by or shared with the developer.

Discord community

The Overload Discord server is an optional third-party service run by Discord Inc. Anything you post there is subject to Discord's privacy policy, not this one.

This website

overload-strength-tracker.app uses Google Analytics to count visits and understand which pages people read. It records standard information such as pages viewed, approximate region and browser type. It is used only in aggregate, it is not linked to anything in the app, and it has no access to your training data.

Children

Overload is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Material changes will also be announced in the Discord community.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else in Overload, are welcome in the Overload Discord.
